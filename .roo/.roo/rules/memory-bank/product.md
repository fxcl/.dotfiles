# Product Description

## Problem Statement

Developers often perform a variety of distinct tasks that require different cognitive approaches and toolsets, such as writing new code, debugging, refactoring, or writing documentation. A generic AI assistant may not be optimized for the specific context of each task.

## Solution

RooCode is a VS Code extension that provides a suite of specialized AI modes, each tailored for a specific development task. This allows developers to switch to the most appropriate AI assistant for their immediate needs, improving the quality and efficiency of the AI-assisted workflow.

## User Experience

From a user's perspective, RooCode should feel like having a team of specialized AI experts available directly within their editor. The experience should be seamless, with intuitive mode-switching and consistent behavior across all modes. The goal is to make AI assistance a natural and powerful part of the development process.
